# EveryTab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 11, 2026_

EveryTab is a local-first Chrome extension for managing browser tabs. The
extension handles information about tabs on your device so its features can
work. The EveryTab developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share that
information.

## Information handled by the extension

EveryTab uses tab addresses, titles, favicons, last-used timing, window
placement, and pinned or grouped state to show, save, search, organize, suspend,
snooze, deduplicate, close, and restore tabs.

It also stores information you add to EveryTab, including Labels, Sessions,
Groups, Starred pages, settings, optional New Tab phrases, and the local Activity
journal.

If you grant the optional Browser History permission, EveryTab uses matching
history entries locally for Search and suggestions. If you grant the optional
Chrome tab-group permission, EveryTab uses group names, colors, and collapsed
state locally when saving and restoring Sessions. You can revoke either
permission in EveryTab Settings.

EveryTab does not read page bodies, form text, passwords, cookies, personal
messages, or other page content. It has no host permissions or content scripts.

## Where the information is stored

EveryTab's working library and recovery data are stored in Chrome's local
IndexedDB on your device. Smaller settings and browser-coordination records use
Chrome's local extension storage.

You may optionally connect a Vault folder that you choose. The Vault contains
compact incremental data, recovery points, favicons, and readable views of your
library. EveryTab can read and write only the folder you select. If you use
Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud, Syncthing, or another service to sync that folder,
that service handles the folder under its own terms and privacy policy. The
EveryTab developer never receives the folder or its contents.

## Web search

EveryTab searches your local tab library first. Only when you choose
**Search web** does the extension ask Chrome to search through your configured
search provider. EveryTab does not send the query to an EveryTab server, receive
it after the handoff, or add it to your EveryTab library. Your search provider
handles the query under its own privacy policy.

## Data collection, sharing, and advertising

EveryTab has no account system, developer-operated server, analytics,
advertising, or telemetry. The EveryTab developer cannot see your tab library or
the contents of your Vault.

EveryTab does not sell or transfer user information to third parties. It does
not use tab information for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or any
purpose unrelated to the tab-management features you choose.

## Retention and control

Saved items remain in your local library until you delete them or a retention
setting you chose moves or removes them. Recovery data and automatic Snapshots
use the limits selected in Settings. You can export your saved data from
EveryTab, delete individual items in the app, remove the extension's local data
by uninstalling it through Chrome, and delete an optional Vault folder using
your file manager.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web
Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes to this policy

If EveryTab's data practices change, the updated behavior will be disclosed
before it starts and this policy will be updated with a new date.
