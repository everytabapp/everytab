# EveryTab

**Every tab, kept, organized, local.**

EveryTab is a free, local-first Chrome extension for saving, organizing,
suspending, snoozing, and finding browser tabs. It replaces your New Tab page
with a complete Dashboard, a Minimal page, or a nearly Blank page.

EveryTab uses tab titles, addresses, favicons, and the organization you add so
these features can work. The data stays on your device unless you connect a
Vault folder and sync it with your own file service. EveryTab has no account,
server, analytics, advertising, or telemetry.

## Why EveryTab

- **Close tabs with confidence.** Anything EveryTab closes can first be kept in
  the Inbox, a Session, the Archive, or Snoozed until later.
- **Keep projects together.** Sessions preserve windows, Labels organize saved
  tabs, and Groups organize Labels and Sessions.
- **Find anything quickly.** Search open and saved tabs by text, domain, source,
  Label, or Session. Browser History is available only if you grant access.
- **Make room for the work that matters.** Suspend inactive tabs, close
  duplicates, review Close the day, and use Focus when you need it.
- **Make it yours.** Choose from 36 built-in color schemes in Light and Dark,
  create your own, and use a Soft, Crisp, or Terminal interface.

## Privacy

EveryTab stores your tab library locally and sends nothing to an EveryTab
service because no such service exists. If you explicitly choose Search web,
Chrome sends the query to your configured search provider as a normal browser
search. Read the full [privacy policy](./privacy).
