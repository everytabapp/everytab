# EveryTab trademark policy

The EveryTab source code is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
That license covers code. It does not grant rights to the project's identity.

The following identify the official EveryTab project and its releases:

- the name "EveryTab" and the EveryTab wordmark,
- the pointed teal stack-and-keeper mark,
- the product icons shipped in `extension/` (`icon*.png`) and their source
  files under `extension/test/ci-evidence/`.

They are not licensed under MPL-2.0 or any other open-source license, even
though the icon files live in this repository so that the unmodified extension
can be built and tested.

## What you may do without asking

- Use the code under MPL-2.0, including in a modified or commercial product.
- Say truthfully that your work is "based on EveryTab", "forked from EveryTab"
  or "compatible with EveryTab", as long as you do not suggest that it is
  official or endorsed.
- Write about EveryTab, review it, teach with it and link to it.
- Use the unmodified name and mark to refer to the official extension, for
  example in a screenshot or a blog post.

## What a fork or modified build must do

- Choose its own product name. Do not call it EveryTab or a name that is
  confusingly similar (for example "EveryTab Pro", "EveryTabs" or "EvryTab").
- Replace the icons and the mark with its own. Do not reuse the pointed teal
  stack-and-keeper mark or the EveryTab icons as the identity of the fork.
- Not present itself as an official EveryTab release, and not publish to a
  store, package registry or domain under the EveryTab name.
- Keep the MPL-2.0 license and copyright notices in the covered files, and
  keep attribution to EveryTab in text if it wishes.

## Official releases

The official EveryTab release is the one published by the maintainers on the
Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/ (listing link to be
updated at launch). Source for each release is tagged in this repository with
its version number.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, or you want permission for something
not covered here, email everytabapp@gmail.com. We try to say yes to honest,
non-confusing uses.
