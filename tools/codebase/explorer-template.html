A desk, a filing cabinet,
and a backup folder.

Your tabs are cards on a desk. Inbox is the tray for later. Sessions are named sets of cards. Labels are stickers. Snooze adds an alarm clock. Snapshots remember what was open. Vault keeps a second copy in your own folder.

Saving, closing a browser tab, and copying a file are separate steps. The code needs receipts and fresh identity checks between them. Cards remember addresses, not every unfinished form on a page.
